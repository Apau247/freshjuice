Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
Factory Management System for Fresh Fruit Juice Production Factory
1. Introduction
The system manages procurement, production, quality, inventory, maintenance, compliance, sales and reporting for a fresh fruit juice production factory.
2. Purpose
To automate factory operations, improve efficiency, reduce wastage and ensure quality and regulatory compliance.
3. Scope

•	Production

•	Finished goods
•	Sales
•	Staff
